Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Step 1

Learn the basic positions and court rules. Understand rotation and serving techniques before playing your first match.

Step 2

Master the fundamentals. Keep your knees bent, ready your platform for passing/bumping, practice setting, and learn accurate serving techniques.

Step 3

Focus on communication and team play. Always call out the ball, prioritize control over power, and strive for accurate passes to your setter.
